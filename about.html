ABOUT THE BLOG

Learning AI, One Idea at a Time.

AI Insights is a simple student blog created to explore Artificial Intelligence in an easy-to-understand way.

Why this blog?

Artificial Intelligence is growing quickly and affects many areas of modern life. This blog presents basic concepts, applications, benefits and challenges of AI in simple language.

The goal is to make technology easier to understand for students and beginners while connecting AI with real-world examples such as education and digital marketing.
